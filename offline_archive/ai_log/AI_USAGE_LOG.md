### 1. What did AI help me accomplish?

### 2. What did I personally verify?

### 3. What did I learn from using AI?
I learned that AI is a productivity accelerator, not a developer substitute. Real engineering value lies in domain architecture, defensive security, edge-case validation, and the ability to diagnose subtle runtime errors that AI models consistently overlook.

### 4. What limitations/errors did I discover in AI-generated answers?
I uncovered three notable hallucinations:

- Suggested complex floating-point mathematical structures exceeded the RAM capacity of 8-bit ATmega328P microcontrollers.

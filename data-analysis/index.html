Statistical Proof

Strong Positive Correlation: r = 0.7556

Coefficient Interpretation:

The computed Pearson Correlation Coefficient between Internet Hours and Total Sales is 0.7556 ($R^2 \approx 0.571$).

This indicates a robust, statistically significant positive relationship: as customers stay longer at internet stations, their likelihood of purchasing supplementary high-margin services (such as document printing, thesis scanning, and gaming) multiplies exponentially.

Business Takeaway:
Extending customer session duration is the primary commercial engine of the computer shop.
